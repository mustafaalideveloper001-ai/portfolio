About Me

Hi, I'm Mustufa — a Shopify developer, not a general web guy

I work inside the Shopify ecosystem full-time — Liquid, theme sections, the Storefront API, and everything in between. My job isn't to make a store look nice in a screenshot; it's to make sure it loads fast, holds up when you add a new collection, and actually converts.

Most of my work lives inside frameworks like T4S/Kalles and other OS 2.0 themes — building custom sections, fixing broken JavaScript, wiring up apps properly, and cleaning up code that's grown messy over time. I'd rather ship something that works reliably than something that only looks good in a demo.

I'm currently working as a Shopify Developer with Zubaidas Little Darling Online. Outside of client work, I spend time reading through Shopify's changelog and testing new theme architecture — usually figuring out why a section broke at 2am.

{% section: services %}

What I Do

Shopify work, specifically — no design-agency add-ons.

Theme Customization & Design

I take your existing theme — Kalles, Dawn, T4S, or anything else — and shape it around your brand. Custom layouts, color systems, and section arrangements that don't look like a template with a new logo.

Custom Sections & Features

Need something your theme doesn't have out of the box? I build custom sections and snippets from scratch with Liquid, JavaScript, and CSS — sticky cart drawers, custom filters, whatever your store actually needs.

App Integration & Store Setup

From payment gateways to review apps to inventory tools, I handle setup and integration so everything talks to each other properly — no half-working app clashing with your theme code.

Speed Optimization & Bug Fixing

Slow load times and broken sections cost you sales. I audit your store's code, clean up render-blocking scripts, and fix the bugs that showed up after the last "quick update."

Shopify SEO & Store Audit

A full pass over your store's structure — page titles, image alt text, URL handles, broken links, and technical SEO issues that come from theme code, not just content.

Store Migration

Moving from WooCommerce, Wix, or another platform to Shopify. Products, collections, customer data, and content get moved over cleanly, with a theme built to match your brand from day one.
