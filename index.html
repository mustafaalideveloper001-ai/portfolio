06 / BEFORE WE START

GOOD QUESTIONS.
CLEAR ANSWERS.

Can you improve my existing store?+

Yes. I can work with your existing Shopify theme, build custom sections, fix bugs or refresh the layout. A full rebuild is only needed when it fits your goals.

How long does a project take?+

Small customizations usually take a few days. A full theme customization or store setup typically takes 2–4 weeks, depending on scope. We'll agree on the timeline before starting.

Will I own the custom code?+

Yes. The custom code and sections I build belong to your store and can be used and edited after the project.

What happens after launch?+

The Custom Build includes two weeks of post-launch support. The Full Store Build includes 30 days of priority support. Ongoing work can be discussed separately.
